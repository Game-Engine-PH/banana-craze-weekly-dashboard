1📊Executive Summary Aug 17-Aug 23

Overall assessment: The report covers Aug 17 to Aug 23. BANANA CRAZE recorded ₱146,600.00 in bets, ₱137,996.25 in payouts, ₱8,603.75 GGR, and 94.13% RTP. 0 unsettled records were identified.

BingoPlus Platform access: BANANA CRAZE did not have a visible entry point on the BingoPlus Platform during the reporting week. Players could access it only by manual search. Verify this access issue before judging game performance.

Business: Weekly GGR was positive at ₱8,603.75. Daily RTP stayed close to the 97.00% reference on most days. Aug 18 reached 102.20% because activity was low; this is short-term variance, not a confirmed payout problem. Weekly RTP ended at 94.13%, below the 97.00% reference, mainly because payouts were lower on Aug 21 and Aug 23. Most rounds were losing rounds (70.17%), which helped keep the weekly RTP below the reference.

Players: The report had 167 New Users (81.86%) and 37 Old Users (18.14%). This confirms that more New Users played than returning players, even without a visible BANANA CRAZE entry point on the BingoPlus Platform. Most New Users stopped before reaching 20 rounds (100.00% stop rate), compared with 84.62% for players who reached 51+ rounds. The main concern is early-session drop-off. The data suggests that player losses may be part of the reason, but it does not prove the cause.

Game results: Most rounds were losing rounds (70.17%). Only 348 rounds reached at least 10x, with a maximum of 75x. Larger wins came from a smaller number of high-multiplier rounds.

Bonus features: The report recorded 305 Bonus Trigger rounds, 0 Mission Feature rounds, and 58 BUY FEATURE rounds. BUY FEATURE contributed ₱10,712.50 in payout. Bonus activity came mainly from regular betting triggers, while BUY FEATURE was less frequent but still contributed a measurable payout. Mission Feature activity was absent, so no Rich Card activity affected the weekly feature result.

Management action: There was no weekly loss event because GGR was positive. The main items that need management attention are low discoverability, early New User drop-off, and the weekly RTP result below the 97% reference. These are monitoring items, not confirmed incidents. The immediate business action is to restore or confirm a visible BANANA CRAZE entry point on BingoPlus. Until that is verified, low traffic cannot be fairly attributed to player rejection of the game.
