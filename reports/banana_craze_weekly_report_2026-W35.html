Daily Status is graded against the real production Fuse Breaker alert formula (production_fuse_monitor.py), not an invented scale — so it only escalates when the actual thing that pages the team would have fired. Checked over every rolling 12-hour window in the day (13 possible windows):
  • Warning = (small-bet winning loss ≥₱20,000 OR large-bet winning loss ≥₱60,000) AND RTP ≥102% AND 12h GGR loss ≥₱20,000.
  • Melt Candidate = the same formula with a ≥₱80,000 12h GGR loss (bet <₱200 counts as “small,” ₱200 and above is “large” — the exact BET_SPLIT constant used in production, correcting an earlier ₱15 figure that didn't match the live code).
  • Critical Review = a real ≥₱20,000 12h GGR loss occurred but RTP and/or winning-loss concentration stayed below the full formula — worth a look, not an alert.
  • Watch = benign statistical drift only (RTP outside the 90–96% band, or rounds ≥50% day-over-day) with no real GGR-loss signal.
  • Normal = none of the above.
  • None of week 36's 7 active days actually crossed the Warning or Melt gates — the worst 12h GGR loss all week was −₱5,445.00 (2026-09-01), well under the ₱20,000 Warning floor, so nothing here would have triggered a real escalation.
  • Hit Rate = rounds where payout > bet, divided by all rounds.
  • Top 5 Payout Share = the top 5 players' combined payout that day, divided by the day's total payout.
  • RTP Adjust Events = rounds where the house RTP-adjustment engine (adjustType) fired to something other than NON.
